The City

Davao City is the capital of the Davao region on the island of Mindanao. The city can be divided in two parts, the old town and the modern city.

Davao Region consists of five provinces, namely: Davao de Oro, Davao del Norte, Davao Oriental, Davao del Sur and Davao Occidental.
